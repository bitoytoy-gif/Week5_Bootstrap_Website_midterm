History & Significance

Mayon Volcano, standing at 2,462 meters, is famous for its perfect cone shape. It is an active stratovolcano located in Albay, Philippines. Mayon is deeply rooted in Filipino folklore and has erupted more than 50 times in recorded history.

The volcano is a major tourist attraction, offering breathtaking views, hiking adventures, and cultural significance to the locals.
